Session 01 · Autonomous Drone Obstacle Detector

Inside one artificial neuron

An autonomous quadcopter reads onboard range and velocity sensors every millisecond and decides whether to hold course or trigger an evasive climb. Strip away the buzzwords: that decision is a dot product, a bias shift, and a non-linear gate.
